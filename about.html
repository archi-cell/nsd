Director's Profile

A Veteran Educator & Skill Development Specialist

I am Navdeep Singh Dadhwal, currently working as a Centre Manager at a Multinational Education-based company, and the Founder-Director of NSD Computer Institute. With over 20+ years of rich experience in conducting Government-recognized computer trainings and 13 years of specialized mathematics coaching for students from 6th to 10th standards, my mission has always been to make premier education reachable to every ambitious individual.

Over the span of my career, I have served in prestigious international and national bodies, notably as an ICT Officer with the Commonwealth (International Organization) and as a Senior Computer Faculty at the Rajiv Gandhi National Institute of Youth Development (RGNIYD Regional Centre). Through government skill initiatives like PMKVY and non-PMKVY schemes with NSDC and Sector Skill Councils (IT, Telecom, Tourism & Hospitality, Healthcare), I have personally trained over 4,000+ students who now contribute dynamically to the modern workforce.

Vision, Mission & Core Values

The foundational principles that drive every lecture, practical lab session, and student interaction at NSD.

Our Vision

We envision a future where excellence knows no bounds. We aspire to be the catalysts of change, shaping a tomorrow where individuals are equipped with the skills, knowledge, and mindset to conquer challenges and embrace limitless possibilities. Our vision is a world where every person can realize their true potential and contribute to building a brighter, more prosperous future for all.

Our Mission

We are dedicated to transforming lives through cutting-edge technical training, comprehensive soft skills development, and robust capacity building. Our unwavering mission is to empower individuals to seize their full potential and excel in the workforce, while enriching their lives with endless opportunities for growth, financial independence, and success.

Core Values

Integrity, accessibility, hands-on rigor, and relentless student support define our institution. We reject passive rote learning in favor of real workstation practice, ensuring that every concept taught directly enhances employability, problem-solving confidence, and career resilience.
